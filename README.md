# PROPULSION-4TH-SEM
CFD Modelling of Thermal Stratification in Uninsulated Cryogenic Propellant Tanks (LPSC)
Summary
Work: Employed the Volume of Fluid method to capture sloshing, evolution of tank temperature and the mass transfer dynamics of the propellant due to aerodynamic loads at lift-off.
Results:
Built a 3D transient VOF-based CFD model (ANSYS Fluent, SST k-omega turbulence, Mechanistic Lee model) of an uninsulated LOX tank under coupled aerothermal heating and launch vehicle slosh excitation, capturing stratification, boundary layer quenching, and ullage pressurization dynamics.
Quantified a 60K thermal stratification gradient between bulk liquid (350K) and ullage vapor (410K), and obtained a heat transfer split of 100-135 W/m²K in wetted/boiling zones and 30-60W/m²K in dry/vapor zones, driven by slosh induced boundary layer breakdown.
Validated simulation outputs against 8 independent parameters from NASA K-Site data and NIST REFPROP (peak vapor temperature, phase change mass transfer rate, turbulent kinetic energy, heat transfer coefficients for wet and dry regions, specific heat, thermal conductivity, mixture enthalpy), achieving agreement within 5-10% across all of them.
